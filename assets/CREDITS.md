# Asset credits

Sources, licences and checksums for every downloaded file are in [provenance.json](provenance.json). Files derived from
them and served by the app are in [derived-provenance.json](derived-provenance.json). The courtyard and the base
characters use CC0 sources, including Ayanda's olive top, which is the casual suit from MakeHuman's CC0 system assets.
One garment is CC BY and needs this credit wherever the advisor is shown or distributed.

## CC BY garment in the fictional draft characters

- **The advisor's navy overshirt** adapts `F_Cardigan_Long_Open_Front` by Mindfront (Sweden), [CC BY
  4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), from the MakeHuman `shirts02` pack. Changes: recoloured, shortened and fitted.

Ayanda's former work shirt, a CC BY garment whose exact licence version was not confirmed, has been removed.

The characters, apron and animations were assembled for Parallax. They are fictional and are not accepted as photoreal.
