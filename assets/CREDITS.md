# Asset credits

Sources, licences and checksums for every downloaded file are in [provenance.json](provenance.json). Files derived from
them and served by the app are in [derived-provenance.json](derived-provenance.json). The courtyard and the base
characters use CC0 sources. The two garments below are CC BY and need this credit wherever the characters are shown or
distributed.

## CC BY garments in the fictional draft characters

- **Ayanda's olive shirt** adapts `mens_shirt_untuck_elvbhp1f` by Elvaerwyn, from the MakeHuman `shirts02` pack
  (<https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/shirts02/shirts02_ccby.zip>). The source `.mhclo` and the
  [pack listing](https://www.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shirts02.html) say `CC_by` with no version, so the
  exact version is **not confirmed**. Changes: recoloured and fitted to the character.
- **The advisor's navy overshirt** adapts `F_Cardigan_Long_Open_Front` by Mindfront (Sweden), [CC BY
  4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), from the same pack. Changes: recoloured, shortened and fitted.

The characters, apron and animations were assembled for Parallax. They are fictional and are not accepted as photoreal.
