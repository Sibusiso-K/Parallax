# Asset credits

The demo portraits `web/public/avatars/A.png`, `B.png` and `C.png` are attributed to Muse Image in commit
`3a9f37b87aff9106b62c6a5e09d6c7264d70c976` (2 November 2026). Their hashes and that attribution are recorded in
`provenance.json`. The repository evidence does not include a generation receipt or an exact model version.
These portraits are separate from the MakeHuman draft characters below.

Sources, licences and checksums for every downloaded file are in [provenance.json](provenance.json). Files derived from
them and served by the app are in [derived-provenance.json](derived-provenance.json). The courtyard and the base
characters use CC0 sources, including Ayanda's olive top, which is the casual suit from MakeHuman's CC0 system assets.
One garment is CC BY and needs this credit wherever the advisor is shown or distributed. The room's caption carries it
whenever the characters are shown (`?people`).
